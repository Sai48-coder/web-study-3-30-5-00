Pizza

Pizza is a globally beloved dish featuring a yeasted flatbread typically topped with savory tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and various meats or vegetables.

Burger

The burger is a global icon of comfort food, celebrated for its satisfying textures and endless variety. At its heart, a perfect burger relies on the contrast between a savory, seared patty and a soft, toasted bun that holds everything together.

HotDog

The hotdog is a quintessential piece of street food history, valued for its portability and snap. While often associated with ballparks and summer cookouts, this simple sausage tucked into a sliced bun serves as a blank canvas for regional culinary traditions.
